---
title: "Connection revoked"
description: "What the `connection_revoked` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `connection_revoked` with HTTP 403. Repeating the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

Remove the ohmyho.st connector from your agent and add it again to reconnect.

If the steps above do not resolve it, [report it](/feedback) with the `request_id` from the error.
