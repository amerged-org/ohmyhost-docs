---
title: "Database migrate paid required"
description: "What the `database_migrate_paid_required` error means and what to do next."
---

The API, CLI and MCP return `database_migrate_paid_required` with HTTP 409. Repeating the same request unchanged does not help.

## What to do

A Free workspace has no Dev database. Call database_migrate with environment prod and the same commit_sha, or ask the organization Owner to activate Paid through billing checkout and then migrate Dev. The saved version and its files are unchanged.

If the steps above do not resolve it, [report it](/feedback) with the `request_id` from the error.
