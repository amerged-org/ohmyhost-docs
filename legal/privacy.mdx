For service diagnosis, ohmyho.st uses bounded, private operational measurement of sign-in and onboarding events. The measurement excludes passwords, prompts, raw URLs and IP addresses; an approximate request country may be recorded when available. Detailed events are retained for 30 days, domain facts and activity for 180 days, and coarse daily cohorts for up to 13 calendar months. Access to account identity and campaign attribution requires separate authorization and is audited. An absent event or incomplete history does not establish why a customer did not finish a step. See [campaign links and sign-in help](/quickstart#campaign-links). The full notice linked above governs privacy requests and processing details.

Authenticated remote MCP tool and API calls also produce minimized operational records: the operation, pseudonymous account and workspace references, timing, status and safe error codes. They exclude call arguments, results, URLs, emails and tokens. Authorized operators can link the references to an account. Records are normally exported from the operational buffer to private storage on a 24-hour cycle and expire there 90 days after upload. If export fails, records remain in the buffer until export succeeds. Account deletion also triggers removal, subject to provider availability.
